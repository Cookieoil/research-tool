## Abstract

This paper is the capstone synthesis of the entire research task, which ran on two tracks. Track A (industrial policy) found that Vietnam's rapid growth is FDI-led - foreign firms are ~73% of exports and domestic supply-chain participation fell from 35% to 18% (2009-2023) - with SOE privilege, a finance/land regime that disadvantages private firms, and institutional capacity as the binding drag; five structural tests (FDI trap, reform paradox, capital misallocation, external shock, power/demographics) frame the upgrade. Track B (the game industry) found a ~$817M domestic market with 58.5 million players inside a world-leading mobile export machine (9.6bn downloads in 2024) that earns only ~$0.06 ARPDAU ex-ads; Decree 147/2024 forced foreign publishers to localise or exit, with real-name checks, 16+ rating and under-18 playtime limits, while the state targets $1bn by 2030 and games are a priority cultural industry. Across both tracks the same pattern recurs: an export-factory model with thin value capture, governed by 'controlled liberalisation', bound by execution capacity, and exposed to external dependence. The decision architecture runs from Politburo Resolution 57 through the National Assembly (Resolution 176/2025), the Government (Decree 147), the lead ministry (MIC/ABEI, then MOST and MCST/ABEI) and enforcement. Reform is asymmetric: promotion-side incentives are likely, but deep content/data liberalisation and an end to the local-entity rule are unlikely before 2030-2031. The synthesis is interpretive and draws on the eleven underlying papers plus a machine-readable scorecard.

## 1. Scope and methodology

Program-level synthesis. It consolidates eleven finalized papers (two on industrial policy, seven on the game industry, a series synthesis and a scorecard) plus a dashboard. Evidence includes World Bank reports (Trading Up, Breaking Through, country overview), the IMF country page, official Vietnamese sources (Resolution 57, the 14th Congress Resolution, the game portal), the USTR framework, and trade press (Digital in Asia, VietnamNet, Vietcetera, VietnamNews). Findings are grouped by track and theme; synthesis judgements are labelled analyst_assessment. The master scorecard (15 forecasts + 10 reform predictions + 5 impact channels) lives in scorecard v2 JSON. No new primary data were collected.
